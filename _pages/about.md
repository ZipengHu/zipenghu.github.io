I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics (KIAA), Peking University, where I hold a Boya Fellowship. I received my Ph.D. in Astronomy and Astrophysics from the Australian National University (2024), following an M.S. from ANU (2020) and a B.S. in Physics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (2018).

My research focuses on how molecular clouds form, evolve, and turn into stars, with particular interest in the interplay between magnetic fields, gravity, and turbulence across scales. I combine numerical simulations with observational analysis to study topics such as molecular cloud structure, star-formation efficiency, magnetic-field measurements, and galactic-scale filaments. I am especially interested in how physical processes at small scales connect to galaxy-wide patterns of star formation.

Outside astronomy, I enjoy playing League of Legends, reading history and Chinese classics, and I am always keen to try new food wherever I go. I have an 8-year-old cat who has travelled across the world with me.
